DESCRIPTION
In 1947, General Roger Ramey held a typed message that people still argue over. We gave the high-resolution scans to Claude Opus 5.5 and TypeSafe’s Jev. Ten human readers agree on only 5 of its 74 words. The image method can’t even find a phrase nobody disputes: FORT WORTH, TEX. ranked 183rd of 601. Blurred letters buried in film grain defeat it, so VICTIMS stays a reading, not a fact.
